# My Research

My whole career has been shaped by **interdisciplinarity** - my PhD was in Political Science, my postdocs in Sociology and Political Communication. In my current role, I also cover Corporate Communication. This results in a broader interest in **political communication of different types of organizations** with a specific focus on the **European Union**. Moreover, I started to use **computational methods** of data collection and text analysis in my PostDoc project 'Tango on a Tightrope' (see below) which has since ignited a broader interest in the opportunities and challenges that digital technologies pose for society and research. In the following, I list current and completed (externally funded) projects.



        
## Current projects

### The (In)visible Research Agenda (2024–present)

This developing research line investigates information invisibility and diversity in algorithmically curated information environments. Together with a team of researchers at the University of Amsterdam’s [Amsterdam School of Communication Research (ASCoR)](https://ascor.uva.nl/), [Department of Media Studies](https://www.uva.nl/en/discipline/media-studies/media-studies.html), and [Institute for Logic, Language & Computation](https://www.illc.uva.nl/), led by [Saurabh Khanna](https://www.uva.nl/en/profile/k/h/s.khanna/s.khanna.html), we aim to develop a conceptual and empirical understanding of its different dimensions and manifestations.

The research line is funded by a collaborative ASCoR grant (€25,000) and a midsize grant (€200,000) within the UvA’s [Theme-based Collaboration Scheme](https://www.uva.nl/en/about-the-uva/about-the-university/strategy/theme-based-collaboration/theme-based-collaboration.html).

### Twi-XL (2020–present)

[Twi-XL](https://twi-xl.humanities.uva.nl/) develops digital infrastructure that enables researchers in the social sciences and humanities to systematically examine current and emerging public debates on key societal issues in the Netherlands.

The project brings together an archive of Dutch Twitter/X data (2011–2023), the archive of [Sound & Vision](https://www.beeldengeluid.nl/), and the archive of the [Royal Library of the Netherlands](https://www.kb.nl/). Twi-XL is part of a broader effort to build digital research infrastructure in the Netherlands, funded through the temporary Platform Digital Infrastructure. I serve as project coordinator.




## Previous projects

### Tango on a Tightrope — An Investigation of Media–Politics Dynamics in Ten Years of EU Crises (October 2018–September 2022)

[Tango on a Tightrope](https://www.researchgate.net/project/Tango-on-a-Tightrope-An-Investigation-of-Media-Politics-Dynamics-in-EU-Crisis-Management) investigated media–politics dynamics during ten years of European Union crises. The project examined executives’ crisis communication through press releases and news-media coverage of EU crises to understand which actors lead this interaction. Data collection and analysis relied on computational approaches, including web-scraping routines and automated content analysis.

The project was conducted at the University of Vienna’s [Computational Communication Science Lab](https://compcommlab.univie.ac.at/), led by [Hajo Boomgaarden](http://www.hajoboomgaarden.com/Hajo_Boomgaarden/Work.html) and [Annie Waldherr](https://compcommlab.univie.ac.at/team/annie-waldherr/). It was funded by the Austrian Science Fund’s [Hertha Firnberg Programme](https://www.fwf.ac.at/de/forschungsfoerderung/fwf-programme/firnberg-programm/) (€234,210; grant [T-989](https://list.fwf.ac.at/de/wissenschaft-konkret/im-fokus-firnbergrichter/firnberg-liste/2018/258188)). I served as principal investigator.

A complementary study of Austria was funded by the Austrian National Bank’s [Anniversary Fund](https://www.oenb.at/en/About-Us/Research-Promotion/The-OeNB-Anniversary-Fund.html) (€111,000; grant 18120). I served as co-principal investigator with Hajo Boomgaarden. Tobias Heidenreich and Nina Kriegler were collaborators.

### DeCon — Dynamics of Public Democracy Contestation (August 2023–January 2025)

DeCon was funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) through the [Open Competition SSH XS funding scheme](https://www.nwo.nl/en/news/121-promising-research-projects-launched-open-competition-ssh-xs-funding) (€50,000). The project implemented a pilot study to map and explain public democracy contestation in Dutch and US online news.

We explored whether the expert questionnaire of the Varieties of Democracy project could provide a blueprint for developing resources for automated content analysis—including seed words and supervised machine-learning approaches—to trace the visibility of democratic principles over time.

### TransSOL — Transnational Solidarity at Times of Crisis (June 2015–May 2018)

From May 2017, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the EU project [TransSOL](https://transsol.eu/), which examined solidarity in Europe and the ways different crises have affected it. My main responsibilities were in [Work Package 5](https://transsol.eu/project/work-packages/): a large-scale comparative media analysis of solidarity claims and Facebook comments during the 2015/2016 “refugee crisis”.

I was a member of the coordinating team, led by [Christian Lahusen](https://transsol.eu/people/consortium/university-of-siegen/) at the University of Siegen. [Project publications are available on the TransSOL website.](https://transsol.eu/outputs/scientific-publications/)

### Contestation of Truth — Public Discourses on Migration in Central Europe in the Post-Truth Era (January 2019–December 2022)

I was co-applicant on this project, led by principal investigator [Alena Kluknavská](https://www.muni.cz/en/people/368265-alena-kluknavska) at Masaryk University, Brno. The project studied contestation of truth in Austria and the Czech Republic across mainstream news media, social media, and alternative right-wing media.

Methodologically, the project began with qualitative manual claims-coding and extended this work to automated analyses of six years of media data. It was funded for three years through the Junior programme of the Czech funding agency. [More information about the project is available on ResearchGate.](https://www.researchgate.net/project/Contestation-of-Truth-Public-Discourses-on-Migration-in-Central-Europe-in-the-Post-Truth-Era)
